# Privacy Policy for Backlogr

**Effective Date: August 12, 2026**

Backlogr ("Backlogr", "we", "us", or "our") is a game backlog and social
tracking application operated by Backlogr Devs. This Privacy Policy explains
what information we collect, why we use it, where it is stored, who processes
it, and the choices available to you when you use the Backlogr mobile app,
the Backlogr website, or related services.

Backlogr is designed as a casual social layer around game tracking. It is not
a messaging service, does not provide an algorithmic public feed, and does not
sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to provide the features you choose to
use. Some information remains only on your device unless you sign in and use
an online feature.

### a. Account and authentication information

When you create or use a Backlogr account, we may collect:

- Your Supabase account identifier and authentication session information.
- Your email address, name, profile image, and other basic details returned by
  the identity provider, where that provider makes them available.
- The provider used to sign in, currently Google or Discord, and the provider
  account identifier.
- A Backlogr username, display name, country/region code, profile visibility,
  onboarding status, and account creation details.

We do not receive or store your Google or Discord password. OAuth access,
refresh tokens, client secrets, and similar provider credentials are not stored
in the Backlogr profile tables. Authentication is handled by the provider and
Supabase Auth.

### b. Profile and user-created content

If you complete a Backlogr profile, we may collect and store:

- Your username, display name, biography, and rich-text biography formatting.
- A selected Backlogr avatar, provider avatar, avatar identifier, or Plus
  custom avatar uploaded by you.
- A selected background or Plus custom background uploaded by you.
- Your public/private/friends-only visibility choices and related privacy
  preferences.
- Connected gaming-platform summaries that you explicitly add, such as a
  platform username, avatar, profile URL, provider identifier, and limited
  metadata.

Profile bios, names, avatars, backgrounds, platform identifiers, activity
events, and other profile content may be visible to other Backlogr users when
you choose a public or friends-only setting. Do not include sensitive personal
information in a public profile or biography.

Backlogr is not currently a messaging service. We do not provide private chat
or user-to-user messaging through the social features described in this
policy.

### c. Backlog, gameplay, and social activity information

Depending on the features you use, we may store or process:

- Game titles, identifiers, platforms, statuses, ratings, notes, playtime,
  lists, wishlist information, screenshots, and related backlog data.
- Play-session start/end times and durations.
- Activity events such as completing a game, rating a game, starting or
  finishing a session, or reaching a streak milestone.
- Friend requests, accepted friendships, blocks, activity visibility, and
  notification records.

Activity visibility is controlled by your settings. Friends-only activity is
intended to be visible only to the relevant mutual friends. Public profile
information is visible according to your selected profile settings.

### d. Device and notification information

For signed-in mobile devices, we may collect:

- A securely stored installation identifier.
- Device platform, device name where available, app version, last-seen time,
  active/revoked status, and a device-specific Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)
  registration token.

FCM tokens are used only to deliver notifications to the appropriate devices.
One account may have more than one registered device. Free accounts may be
limited to two active devices; you can revoke registered devices from the
device-management settings when that feature is available in your app
version.

You can control Backlogr notification categories in notification settings and
can also disable notifications through your operating-system settings.

### e. Subscription and entitlement information

Plus subscriptions and purchases are processed by the applicable app-store or
payment provider, such as Google Play Billing or Apple’s App Store when that
store is supported. We do not collect or store your full payment-card or bank
account details.

We may receive and store subscription identifiers, transaction references,
product/plan information, entitlement status, expiry information, and the
provider that verified the entitlement. This information is used to provide
Plus features such as eligible custom profile media and other product access.

### f. Analytics, diagnostics, and technical information

We may collect technical information needed to operate and improve Backlogr,
including app version, operating system, device type, IP address, crash data,
performance data, logs, and feature usage information. Where enabled, third-
party analytics or diagnostic SDKs may process this information according to
their own privacy policies and the disclosures in the applicable app-store
privacy forms.

We do not use this information for targeted advertising. We will update this
policy before introducing advertising, precise location collection, contacts
access, health data, biometric data, or other materially different collection.

### g. Local storage and offline cache

Backlog data, preferences, session state, cached profiles, and notification
history may be stored locally on your device to support offline use and fast
startup. The mobile app may cache a limited number of recent notifications and
your signed-in profile in local storage. Local caches are not a substitute for
the authoritative server copy and may remain on a device until you sign out,
clear app data, or uninstall the app.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Create, authenticate, maintain, and secure your Backlogr account.
- Provide backlog tracking, profile, friends, activity, notification, and
  cross-device features that you request.
- Display your profile and social content according to your visibility and
  activity settings.
- Store and display profile media you select or upload, subject to Plus
  eligibility and storage rules.
- Register and manage your devices, deliver FCM notifications, and enforce
  device limits.
- Verify subscriptions and manage Plus entitlements.
- Cache data locally so the app works reliably when temporarily offline.
- Prevent abuse, enforce our Terms of Use, investigate security incidents,
  troubleshoot failures, and comply with legal obligations.
- Respond to support requests and communicate important service, security, or
  policy updates.

We do not sell personal information, use profile content for targeted
advertising, or use private profile/activity data to build an advertising
profile.

## 3. How We Share Information

We share information only as needed to operate Backlogr:

- **Supabase:** Supabase Auth, Postgres, Storage, and related infrastructure
  host account, profile, social, device, notification, entitlement, and other
  Backlogr data. Access is protected by authentication and database/storage
  permissions.
- **Google and Discord:** The provider receives authentication requests when
  you choose that provider. We receive the basic identity information that the
  provider returns and store only the Backlogr profile/provider details needed
  for account functionality.
- **Firebase Cloud Messaging:** FCM receives device tokens and notification
  payloads required to deliver push notifications. Backlogr does not place
  Firebase server credentials in the app.
- **Apple, Google Play, and payment providers:** These providers process app
  purchases and provide limited transaction or entitlement information needed
  to verify Plus access.
- **Analytics and diagnostics providers:** Where enabled, technical,
  performance, crash, and usage information may be processed to maintain and
  improve the app.
- **Service providers and legal disclosures:** Trusted providers may process
  information on our behalf. We may disclose information where required by
  law, to protect users or the service, prevent fraud or abuse, or in a merger,
  acquisition, financing, or sale of assets.

We do not share your personal information with unaffiliated third parties for
their own marketing purposes. We do not share your provider passwords,
OAuth client secrets, or payment-card details.

## 4. User-Generated Content and Safety

Backlogr profiles, bios, avatars, backgrounds, platform summaries, activity,
and other social content may be user-generated. You are responsible for the
content you submit and must not submit unlawful, abusive, hateful, sexually
explicit, infringing, deceptive, or privacy-invasive content.

We may review, restrict, remove, or disable access to content or accounts that
violate our Terms of Use, applicable law, or the safety of the service. Users
can report concerns or request review by contacting support@backlogr.site.
Blocking and privacy controls may limit profile, activity, friend, and
notification interactions. Backlogr is not responsible for information that a
user chooses to publish publicly or share outside the service.

## 5. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards,
including authenticated access controls, row/storage permissions, encrypted
connections, and protected server-side credentials. No internet transmission,
cloud service, or local device is completely secure. You are responsible for
protecting your device and keeping your identity-provider account secure.

## 6. Retention and Deletion

We retain account, profile, social, device, notification, subscription, and
other server data for as long as necessary to provide the service, meet legal
and financial obligations, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and enforce our
agreements. Retention periods may differ by data type.

When you request account deletion, we will delete or anonymize the Backlogr
account and associated personal data, subject to information we must retain by
law or for legitimate security, fraud-prevention, dispute, or accounting
purposes. We will also remove or de-identify associated profile, social,
device, notification, activity, and stored-media data where applicable.

Deleting the app alone does not delete a Backlogr account. Account deletion
must be requested through the in-app account controls when available or by
contacting support@backlogr.site from the account email address. We will use
reasonable steps to verify the request and may ask for additional information
to prevent unauthorized deletion. A public web account-deletion request page
will be provided at https://backlogr.site/delete-account before store release
of account-creating versions that require it.

## 7. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to:

- Access, receive, correct, or update personal information.
- Request deletion or restriction of processing.
- Object to or withdraw consent for processing where consent is the legal
  basis.
- Control profile visibility, activity visibility, connected accounts,
  notification categories, device access, and Plus uploads.
- Unlink a connected provider where supported, without deleting unrelated
  Backlogr data.
- Disable push notifications through Backlogr or your device settings.

Contact support@backlogr.site to exercise a right or ask a privacy question.
We may verify your identity before fulfilling a request. You may also have the
right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

## 8. Children’s Privacy

Backlogr is not directed to children under 13, or the higher minimum age
required in a user’s jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal
information from children below the applicable age. If you believe a child has
provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and delete it
where appropriate.

## 9. International Processing

Backlogr Devs is based in Uganda. Our service providers may process
information in Uganda, the United States, the European Union, or other
countries where they operate. Those countries may have different data
protection rules. We use reasonable contractual, technical, and organizational
safeguards appropriate to the service and applicable law.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy when our data practices, features, providers, or
legal obligations change. We will update the effective date and, where
appropriate, provide notice in the app, on the website, or by email. The
current version will be available through Backlogr and at the published policy
URL.
